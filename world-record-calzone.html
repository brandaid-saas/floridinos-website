Let the Record Breaking Begin!

The whole thing started as a way to give back to my community that I so loved. I thought what better way to raise some money for charitable research organizations but by creating a record-breaking calzone.

In 1989, our first attempt was a 13 ft. calzone.

1990
139 FT., 2 IN.

The very next year, with the help of the community and close friends, we were able to extend the record for the World's Largest Calzone to 139 ft., 2 inches. All proceeds went to fighting Multiple Sclerosis.

APRIL 1991
378 FT.

And in April of 1991, I decided to create one more gigantic calzone. With a tremendous amount of community support, we were able to set the record for the World's Largest Calzone by baking a calzone measuring 378 ft. Proceeds were donated to the MS Foundation and the American Cancer Society.

1993
409 FT.

Two years later, in 1993, I decided to go even further. With that same community spirit behind us once again, we set out to break our own record one more time — and succeeded, baking a calzone measuring 409 ft., the largest we had ever made.

The size and record was certified by the Florida Department of Agriculture, Measure and Weights.
