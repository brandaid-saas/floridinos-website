OUR STORY

CAMPOSANO, ITALY

Rich in Family History while Embracing the American Dream

"I'm very proud of my accomplishments, which all started as a young boy from Italy, coming to America and dreaming of a better life. To us Europeans, the United States was and is the Land of Opportunity, and that in itself was a far-away dream which turned into reality."
— MICHAEL FLORIDINO

My family were farmers from Camposano, a suburb of Napoli, Italy. The whole family would travel to work on my father's motorcycle — me and my brother on the gas tank in front of my father, my mother behind my father holding on to my two sisters.

1971 — PORTLAND, MAINE

Floridino's History — Continued

We worked hard and lived a rich culture of land to table with my family developing the recipes that I still use today. Back then, the thoughts of a better life in America was always on my mind. Even my favorite TV program was American — Rin Tin Tin!

In 1971, when I was 13 years old, my family immigrated to the United States. We first settled in Portland, Maine where I starting working as a shoe shine boy at 14 to help support the family. Shortly after that, I began working in a Pizzeria which lasted for the next 18 years.

FLORIDA, 1988

Floridino's Pizza & Pasta to Floridino's Italian Kitchen

My experience of working in the food industry for many years coupled with my passion for cooking that had developed in all the years of kitchen time with my mother, I decided to strike out on my own. I moved to Florida in November of 1988 and opened Floridino's Pizza & Pasta. I wanted to take homestyle Italian cooking into a casual restaurant setting without sacrificing flavor and quality. All of our dishes are heavily influenced by southern Italy-style cooking.

In 2008, I restructured my whole Italian eatery concept and Floridino's Italian Kitchen was born! New look, new menu, new delicious dishes with even more value for your money! I feel that your home cooking is now our only competition!

I love creating tasty new dishes for my friends, family and ultimately my customers — at the end of the day, we are all one big, happy family!
